07

Your business starts with one main branch. If you have more shops, the main manager adds each one under Branches, with its own address, phone and opening hours. All branches share one price list and one payout bank account, and your public page lists every branch so customers pick the nearest. Hours matter: when a branch is closed, customers can’t place drop-off or pickup orders with it. On a public holiday or a day you can’t open, tap Close for today, and Start taking orders when you’re back. Staff are placed at a branch from the Team page.
