The Queue is your home screen and where every day starts. It has three columns. New and incoming: orders customers have just placed — drop-offs on their way and pickups to collect; staff at that branch also get an email and WhatsApp alert for each one. In progress: orders being checked in or processed. Ready to hand over: finished orders waiting for collection or out with a rider. Tap any order to open it. If you have several branches and see all of them, use the branch buttons at the top to show one branch at a time.
