When an order is ready, it either waits for the customer to collect it or goes out for delivery. For delivery, enter the rider’s name and phone — your own rider or a dispatch partner — and tap Send out for delivery; give the rider the order code. At the door, or at the counter for a collection, the customer shows their 4-digit handover PIN: enter it to close the order. If they chose pay on delivery, they tap Pay now in the app at the door instead — no cash and no code needed; the order closes once the payment comes through. If a delivery doesn’t happen, use the buttons under “Delivery didn’t happen?”: “Customer unavailable” adds a small penalty to their bill, “Missing item / our error” adds none. The order goes back to ready for another attempt.
