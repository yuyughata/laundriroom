At the bottom of every order is its Activity list: when the order was placed, who confirmed the pickup, who checked in each item, who pressed each stage, who sent it out and who handed it over — each with the time. There’s no name to pick from a list; the app simply records whoever is signed in. That’s why every person needs their own login, and why you should sign out on a shared tablet when someone else takes over. When a customer asks about an item, you can see straight away who handled it.
