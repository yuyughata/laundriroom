Once everything is checked in, the order moves through your processing stages. Each time the batch moves on, tap the stage on the tablet: Sorting, Washing, Drying, Pressing, Quality check, Packaging and finally Final completion. Tap them when they actually happen, not all at the end — your customer watches the order move in their app, and that saves you a lot of “is it ready?” calls. Stages go in order and skipping one needs a manager, which makes sure the quality check is never missed. Final completion marks the order ready for collection or delivery.
