laundriroom · Partner Playbook

13

For a pickup order, send your own rider or book a dispatch partner — riders don’t need a laundriroom account. If you book a dispatch partner outside the app, you can log their booking reference on the order. At the door, the rider asks the customer for the 4-digit pickup PIN shown in their app, then phones it through or brings it back, and staff enter it on the order and tap Confirm pickup. Never take clothes without the PIN: it’s your proof the collection happened, and it protects the customer. The activity log records who confirmed it.
