06

Verification is in the menu. First, the main manager reads and accepts the Partner Agreement — the terms you work under on laundriroom. One clause to know well: customers who find you through laundriroom are served through the app for twelve months from their first order with you. If we update the agreement, you’ll be asked to accept the new version. Second, upload a government ID and your CAC certificate. Verification doesn’t stop you trading; once our team has checked them, the “Verified business” badge appears on your page and in the app, and customers trust it.
