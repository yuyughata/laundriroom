14

Check in every garment, one at a time, in the order’s Check in an item box. Attach a tag first, then tap Scan tag and point the tablet camera at the QR code — or type the code. Choose the item type and the service (wash and fold, dry clean, or press only); the price fills in from your price list, and the Add item button also shows what the customer pays. If you see a stain, a tear or wear, write a short condition note and, if you can, take a photo — it’s optional, but it’s your evidence if a customer later says the damage was yours. Tap Add item and move to the next garment. The customer sees each item, its tag and price in their app, and the full total before they pay. Each item records who checked it in.
