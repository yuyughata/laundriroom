laundriroom · Partner Playbook

20

Your business has its own page on laundriroom, created automatically when you joined. It shows your name, branches and hours, your Verified badge, ratings and reviews, and your price list (shown with laundriroom’s commission added — you’re still paid your own price), with an Order button. From My page you can copy the link, send it to WhatsApp in one tap, and download a QR code to print for your counter, your receipts and your flyers. Customers who order through your link are your customers — the page shows how many orders and customers it has brought in. It’s the easiest way to move regulars from phone calls and WhatsApp chats onto tracked, paid app orders.
