A simple routine that keeps everything running. When you open, make sure your branch shows as open and look through New and incoming. Every drop-off: tag and check in each item, noting any damage. Through the day, tap each stage when it really happens and send ready orders out with a rider. At every handover, confirm the PIN — or, for pay on delivery, let the customer pay in the app. At closing, check what’s waiting under Ready to hand over and sign out of the shared tablet. Once a week the main manager reads the reviews, checks there are enough printed tags, logs the week’s expenses and looks over the Ledger.
