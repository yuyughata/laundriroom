laundriroom · Partner Playbook

24

Customers rate each completed order from 1 to 5 stars, and good ratings show on your page and in the app — they’re your best advertising. Reviews shows them all. When a customer gives 1 or 2 stars, you get an email and WhatsApp straight away: look into it, call or message the customer, and put it right quickly. If low ratings keep coming, laundriroom steps in, fairly and in the open: first we ask what happened, and you reply on the Reviews page; if it continues, a formal warning; and only if it still doesn’t improve, suspension from the platform. These standards are what make customers trust every laundry on laundriroom — including yours.
