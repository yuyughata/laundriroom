laundriroom · Partner Playbook

08

The main manager adds staff from Team. Enter their name and email, choose Attendant or Manager, and pick the branch they work at, then tap Send invite. laundriroom emails them an invitation from your laundry; they set their own password and sign in. Until they do, the team list shows “Invite sent” — use Resend invite if they can’t find the email. You can change someone’s role or branch at any time, send a login link if they forget their password, and remove anyone who leaves, which takes away their access straight away. Every person needs their own login — that’s how the app knows who checked in or handed over each order. Our team can also add staff for you if you need help.
