19

Sometimes a customer brings something that isn’t on your list — a kaftan, a graduation gown, a large rug. Anyone on the team can request it from Item requests: type the item’s name and, if you can, a suggested price. The main manager sees it waiting and taps Approve or Reject. Once approved, the item joins your list and can be checked in like any other. Keep the request short and specific so the manager can price it quickly.
