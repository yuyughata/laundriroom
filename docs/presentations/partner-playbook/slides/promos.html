The main manager can run your own promotions from Promos. Tap New promo, choose a code customers will type — FOLD10, LEKKIWEEKEND — and set the discount as a percentage (with an optional maximum) or a fixed naira amount. Add a usage limit, a per-customer limit and an expiry date if you want. Share the code on WhatsApp, your page or at the counter. Your own codes are funded by you: the discount comes out of your payout. Discounts laundriroom runs itself — our promos, referral rewards and the bulk discount for big orders — are paid by laundriroom, so you still get paid in full on those.
