laundriroom · Partner Playbook

05

Everyone signs in on the laundriroom website with their own email and password and lands on the vendor dashboard. Forgot your password? Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in page, or ask your manager to send a login link from Team. The menu on the left is the same for everyone, except that the main manager also sees Team and Ledger. On a phone, the menu sits behind the Menu button at the top. Please never share one login between people — the app records who did each step under the person who is signed in.
