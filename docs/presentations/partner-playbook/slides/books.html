The Ledger is your bookkeeping, filled in from your orders automatically — and only the main manager can see it. At the top: income from paid orders, your expenses, the net, and the payout due (your own prices, in full), plus income split by branch. Use Add expense to record your costs — diesel for the generator, detergent, hangers and bags — and attach a photo of the receipt. The daily cash-up shows each day’s income against expenses, and every payout is listed with its fee, the net paid and its status. Statement, Payouts and Transactions download as PDFs whenever your accountant or bank asks.
