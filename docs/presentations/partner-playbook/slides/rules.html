Six rules that keep you, your customers and laundriroom safe. Tag every item — no tag, no check-in. Always use the PIN — no clothes are collected or handed over without the customer’s 4-digit PIN. Record damage — a condition note and a photo at check-in are your evidence. Everyone uses their own login — never share passwords, because the app records who did each step. Keep your hours true — use Close for today when you’re shut, so customers aren’t left waiting. And keep it in the app: under the Partner Agreement, customers who found you through laundriroom are served through the app for twelve months from their first order with you — no side deals, cash payments or direct bookings for them.
