12

Customers who walk in or phone you still go through the app. From the Queue, tap New order. Search for the customer by their full phone number or email; if they’re new, add their name and phone — with an email, they’ll get an invitation to set up their own account. Then choose the branch, how the clothes come in (at the counter or a pickup) and go back (collection or delivery), Standard or Premium, Normal or Express, and whether they pay online once priced or on delivery. Tap Create order & check items in and you go straight to checking items in. The customer gets an order confirmation by email and WhatsApp.
