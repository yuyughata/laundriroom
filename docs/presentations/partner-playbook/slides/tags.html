10

Every garment gets a tag at check-in, so print a stock before you start. The main manager opens Tags, enters how many to make — up to 500 at a time — and taps Generate. Each tag has a QR code and a short code made of your prefix and a number, like FPL-0042; the prefix is yours alone and numbers never repeat. Choose a size — 2.5 cm is the best all-rounder — then print on plain A4. Important: print at 100% or “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”, and check that the ruler at the bottom of the sheet measures 5 cm. Cut along the dashed lines and punch the small ring to attach the tag with a safety pin or tag gun. When an order is closed its tags are freed to use again.
