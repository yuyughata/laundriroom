laundriroom · How an order flows

22

If a vendor remembers six things, make it these. One: a free storefront — their own page, link and QR code from day one. Two: orders organised — whether a customer orders in the app, through their link, walks in or phones, it’s all in one queue, by branch. Three: protection — tags, check-in photos and PINs prove what happened at every handover. Four: paid automatically — each completed order pays out to their bank account. Five: books that keep themselves — income, expenses, cash-up and statements. Six: they keep their full price — laundriroom’s commission is added on top for the customer, and there’s no subscription. Lead with whichever matters most to the vendor in front of you: payment speed for a busy shop, organisation for a growing one, protection for one that has been burned by disputes.
