laundriroom · How an order flows

19

Vendor → My page is the vendor’s marketing kit. The link to their public page is ready to copy, there’s a one-tap WhatsApp share, and a QR code to download and print for the counter or flyers. The page shows how many orders and customers came through that link, so vendors can see it working. Vendor → Promos lets them run their own campaigns: they create a discount code, set how many times it can be used and when it expires, and fund the discount themselves. This is how a vendor moves their existing WhatsApp and walk-in customers onto app orders — tracked, paid in the app and recorded in their books.
