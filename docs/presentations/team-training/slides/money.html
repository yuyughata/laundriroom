laundriroom · How an order flows

14

laundriroom’s commission is added on top of the vendor’s prices — nothing comes off what the vendor charges. A ₦200 shirt with a 30% commission shows the customer ₦260, and the vendor is paid ₦200. The commission is one rate for every vendor and applies to item prices only — not to delivery or premium extras; fill in the current rate before presenting. The order total is the items at those prices, plus any premium extras, the delivery fee, and an express or premium charge if the customer chose those — less any discount. Express and premium are percentages of the vendor’s price, set by laundriroom and kept by laundriroom. The commission is charged on the normal turnaround; when a customer picks express, the express charge replaces the commission. Premium is added on top either way. So with a 30% commission, 20% express and 12.5% premium, a ₦200 item costs ₦260 normal, ₦285 normal premium, ₦240 express and ₦265 express premium — and the vendor gets ₦200 every time. The vendor receives their own item prices, extras and delivery in full. Discounts: on a promo code the vendor creates, the vendor gives up only their share — laundriroom gives up the share on its commission; laundriroom’s own promos, referral rewards and the bulk discount come entirely out of laundriroom’s share. There’s no subscription — vendors only pay when they earn, and their customers carry the commission.
