laundriroom · How an order flows

03

Five roles. The customer uses the phone app. On the vendor side there are two kinds of staff: the manager, who runs the business, and attendants, who work the counter. Riders can be the vendor’s own or a dispatch partner, and they don’t need an account — the customer’s PIN is what confirms a pickup or delivery. laundriroom sits behind every order: payments, automatic payouts, support, claims and quality standards. A business with several shops can also give staff access to just one branch; we’ll come back to that.
