A vendor is one business with one or more branches. Every vendor starts with a main branch; the business-wide manager can add more, each with its own address, phone, opening hours and an open-or-closed switch for days off. The business keeps one price list, one payout bank account and one profile link across all branches, and its public page lists every branch so customers pick the nearest. Staff can be business-wide, seeing everything, or tied to one branch, in which case they only see that branch’s orders and alerts. Attendants who meet an item that isn’t on the price list can request a new item type, and the manager approves it with a price. This is a strong point for growing laundries with more than one shop.
