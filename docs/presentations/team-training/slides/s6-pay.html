laundriroom · How an order flows

10

Once the items are checked in and priced, the customer can pay in the app through laundriroom’s payment partner (Paystack or Flutterwave). Customers who chose pay on delivery still pay in the app — they tap Pay now when the rider arrives — so the vendor never handles cash. Discounts are applied on the bill: promo codes, referral rewards, and an automatic bulk discount for large orders. Who funds a discount matters: platform promos and the bulk discount come out of laundriroom’s share, not the vendor’s; a promo the vendor creates itself is vendor-funded.
