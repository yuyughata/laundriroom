laundriroom · How an order flows

21

Vendor → Reviews shows the rating summary and every review. Each 1- or 2-star review also emails and WhatsApps the vendor straight away, asking them to look into it, contact the customer and resolve it promptly. laundriroom watches for patterns: a vendor is flagged when low ratings repeat — for example three in thirty days, or more than the month before. The process is fair and in the open: first an inquiry asking what happened, which the vendor answers on this page; then a warning if it continues; and only then suspension. Position it to vendors as protection: customers trust the platform because standards are kept, and that trust is what sends good laundries their orders.
