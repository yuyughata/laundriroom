laundriroom · How an order flows

12

Once the order is complete, the customer can rate it from 1 to 5 stars and leave a comment. Good reviews appear on the vendor’s profile page and in the app, which helps them win new customers. A 1- or 2-star review immediately emails and WhatsApps the vendor, asking them to contact the customer and put it right. If something is damaged or missing, the customer reports it on the order; laundriroom’s team resolves it using the check-in photos and notes — the same evidence that protects the vendor. Customers also have a referral code: when a friend completes their first order, the customer gets a reward promo. Behind the scenes, the garment tags on the order are freed automatically for the next one.
