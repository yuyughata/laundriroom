laundriroom · How an order flows

20

The Ledger is the vendor’s bookkeeping, built from their orders automatically. At the top: income from paid orders, expenses, net, and the payout due (their own prices, in full), plus income split by branch. Vendors can log their own costs — diesel for the generator, detergent, hangers — attach receipts, and see a daily cash-up of income against expenses. Every payout is listed with its gross amount, laundriroom’s fee, the net paid and its status. Statement, payouts and transactions reports download as PDFs, ready for an accountant or a bank. For many small laundries this replaces a notebook.
