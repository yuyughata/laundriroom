laundriroom · How an order flows

13

This is the step vendors care about most. As soon as an order is completed and paid, the payout is worked out and sent to the vendor’s bank account automatically through Paystack — there’s nothing to request and no invoice to send. Nothing is deducted: the commission was added on top of the vendor’s prices for the customer, so the vendor receives their own prices, extras and delivery in full. The vendor sets their bank account once, under Ledger → Payout account, and the account name is checked with the bank. Every payout, with its gross, fee and net, shows in the Ledger. Two exceptions to mention if asked: laundriroom can set a short hold window before payouts go out, and a payout on a disputed order is held until the dispute is resolved.
