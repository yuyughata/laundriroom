laundriroom · How an order flows

07

Two ways in. A drop-off customer simply brings the clothes to the branch during its opening hours. For a pickup, the vendor sends a rider — its own, or a dispatch partner it books, and it can log the dispatch booking reference. The customer’s app shows a 4-digit pickup PIN; the rider asks for it and staff enter it on the order to confirm collection. Riders don’t need to download anything or have an account. Why it matters: the customer knows their clothes only go to the right person, and the vendor has proof of every collection.
