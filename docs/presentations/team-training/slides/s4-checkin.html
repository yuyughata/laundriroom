laundriroom · How an order flows

08

This is where the order gets its detail. For each garment the attendant attaches one of the vendor’s printed tags and scans its QR code with the tablet camera — or types the short code, like FPL-0042. They pick the item type and service (wash and fold, dry clean, or press only), and the price fills in from the vendor’s own price list (the button also shows what the customer pays, with laundriroom’s commission added), so every branch charges the same. Then the evidence: condition notes such as “small tear at hem” and photos of stains or damage. The customer sees every item, its tag and price in the app, and the full total before paying. That evidence is what settles a damage claim fairly later on — it protects the vendor from being blamed for damage that was already there.
