laundriroom · How an order flows

06

The order form walks the customer through every choice. Which branch. Standard or Premium service — Premium unlocks extras like stain treatment or a delicate hand-wash. Normal or Express turnaround. How it gets to the shop: drop it off, or have it picked up. How it comes back: collect it, or have it delivered — by the vendor’s own rider or a dispatch partner — to a saved address. The delivery fee is quoted before they book; where distance pricing is switched on it’s worked out from the real driving distance. Then they choose to pay online once the items are priced, or on delivery. Item prices aren’t set yet — staff price each item at check-in. On the vendor side, that branch’s staff get an email and WhatsApp alert straight away, and the order appears in the queue under New and incoming. Staff can also enter orders for walk-in or phone customers, so every order lives in one place.
