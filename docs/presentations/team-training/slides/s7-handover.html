laundriroom · How an order flows

11

When the order is ready, it either waits for collection or goes out for delivery. For delivery, staff assign a rider by name and phone — their own or a dispatch partner — and tap Send out for delivery. The rider carries the order code. The customer’s app shows a 4-digit handover PIN; the rider or the counter confirms it and the order closes. A pay-on-delivery customer simply pays in the app at the door, which closes the order too. If a delivery doesn’t happen, staff reschedule it: when the customer wasn’t available, a small penalty — a share of the delivery fee set by laundriroom — is added to their bill; when it was the vendor’s error, there’s no penalty.
