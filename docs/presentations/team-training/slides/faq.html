laundriroom · How an order flows

23

The questions vendors ask most, with short answers you can give with confidence. Cost: nothing comes off their prices. laundriroom adds its commission — the same rate for every vendor — on top of their item prices for the customer, so a ₦200 shirt at 30% shows the customer ₦260 and the vendor still gets ₦200. Delivery carries no commission, and on an express order the express charge replaces it. Quote the current rate. No subscription. Payment: automatic, to their bank, as soon as an order is completed and paid; laundriroom can set a short hold window, and a disputed order’s payout waits until it’s resolved. Riders: their own, a dispatch partner, or both; riders need no account. Their own customers: yes — share the link or QR, and staff can enter walk-in and phone orders. Damage claims: the check-in photos and condition notes are the evidence. The Partner Agreement: the terms they accept when applying, including non-circumvention — customers introduced through laundriroom are served through the app for twelve months from that customer’s first order. Several shops: each becomes a branch under one account.
