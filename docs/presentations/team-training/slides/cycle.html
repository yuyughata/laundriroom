One order, nine steps

1

Discover

The customer finds a laundry; the vendor shares its link.

2

Order

The customer books; the branch is alerted at once.

3

Pickup or drop-off

A rider collects with a PIN, or the customer drops off.

4

Check-in

Every item tagged, priced and photographed.

5

Processing

Washing to packing, tracked live by both sides.

6

Payment

In the app — online, or at the door on delivery.

7

Handover

Delivered or collected, confirmed with a PIN.

8

Review & aftercare

Ratings, claims and referral rewards.

9

Getting paid

An automatic payout to the vendor’s bank.
