How a vendor comes on board. They fill in the application on laundriroom’s website and accept the Partner Agreement — the terms they work under, including non-circumvention: customers introduced through laundriroom are served through the platform for twelve months. laundriroom reviews the application and approves it, which creates the vendor’s account. Verification doesn’t hold them up: they upload a government ID and their CAC certificate from Vendor → Verification, and once laundriroom checks them the “Verified business” badge appears on their page. Then they add branches, prices and staff, set their payout bank account, and start sharing their link. If the Partner Agreement changes later, vendors are asked to accept the new version.
